  • ImageGenCam — AI Camera on an Orange Pi Zero 2

    Embedded systems · Featured

    A handheld point-and-shoot that doesn't save what it sees — it saves what an AI reimagines. Press the shutter and the photo is sent to an image model with a chosen prompt; the transformed result comes back on the panel a few seconds later. Forked from the Raspberry Pi original and fully ported to the Orange Pi Zero 2.

    • 320×480 SPI display, USB camera soldered to the header, 18650 power pack and an opto-isolated hot shoe
    • Threaded Python controller plus a phone web UI: gallery, prompt presets, Wi-Fi switching and a remote shutter
    • Persistent job queue surviving reboots, a "magic mode" that has an LLM write the prompt, 63 passing tests

    • GPIO

  • PMSM Drive Control — University Project (TX)

    02/2026 – 07/2026 · UTC

    Bringing a permanent-magnet synchronous motor test bench from simulation to working hardware: closed-loop vector control running in real time on a PLECS RT Box, driving a three-phase inverter.

    • Rebuilt the IR2110 gate-driver circuit — the inherited version tied the SD shutdown pin to VDD, keeping the drivers permanently off
    • Fixed the resolver feedback by adding the missing electrical-to-mechanical conversion (3 pole pairs)
    • Integrated the full chain into one real-time model and added phase-current measurement with clamp meters
    • PLECS
    • Power electronics
    • PWM
    • Control

    Motor turning under closed-loop control at low frequency; synchronism above ~10 Hz still to be tuned.

  • Cheese Handling Machine — CAD Design (TN12)

    06/2026 · UTC · with C. Zheng

    A full mechanical assembly designed in Creo: a machine that grips a cheese wheel, turns it and raises or lowers it for handling in a ripening cellar. About 90 parts, from the welded frame down to the standard fasteners, bearings and springs.

    • Clamping through a screw-and-nut mechanism driven by a hand crank
    • Rotation of the wheel between vertical and horizontal via the pivoting gripper arms
    • Vertical travel of the carriages, with a belt-and-pulley transmission
