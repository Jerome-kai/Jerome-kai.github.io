Cheese Handling Machine

A machine that grips a cheese wheel, turns it, and raises or lowers it for handling in a ripening cellar. It was designed in Creo as a complete assembly of about 90 parts, from the welded frame down to the standard fasteners, bearings and springs.

TN12 mechanical design project at the University of Technology of Compiègne (UTC), June 2026, with C. Zheng.

  • All projects

How it works

Three motions, all driven by hand, so the machine needs no power in the cellar:

  • Clamping: a screw and nut mechanism, turned by a hand crank, closes the two gripper arms onto the wheel.
  • Rotation: the arms pivot so the wheel can go from vertical to horizontal.
  • Lifting: the carriages travel up and down the frame through a belt and pulley transmission.

The assembly is fully parametric. The clamping travel, the arm angle and the carriage height are all driven by sketch dimensions, so any position can be posed directly in the model.
